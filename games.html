Taxi Dash

Coffee Regular

Walk-Up

Scaffolding Maze

Stoop Sale
A lamp, a toaster, and a box of tapes. Haggle nicely. The neighbor is watching.
MetroCard Swipe

Deli Counter

Tourist Trap
Get through Times Square without a bus tour, a fake bag, or a flyer for a show you don’t want.
Fire Escape
Dinner on the fire escape. Don’t drop the fork, and wave to the pigeon like you live here.
Crosswalk Chicken
The light is orange and the taxi is rolling. Walk like you mean it.
Laundromat Roulette
You were gone twenty minutes. Your wet clothes are on the table. Get them in the dryer.

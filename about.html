About NYC.exe

NYC.exe is a free games site about everyday life in New York City. Funny little games, short blurbs, pictures you can send to a friend. The kind of site you might have left open in a browser window in 2001.

It started as a school project: a sister site to the old MiniClip homepage. That first version had jokey copy next to each game and tightly cropped pictures of the characters. A later version of the site was mostly lists — latest, hot, A to Z, every game they had. This draft tries to keep both of those things, only every game is about life in the city.

Some of the games you can play now. The rest are still in the shop.

A couple of questions

If you have a minute, I’d like to know:

  • Does this feel like an old games site, or just a copy of one?
  • Should more of the games be playable for the next draft?

If you want your game on the site, send a note through the send to a friend page and say so in the message. No promises, but we’ll look.
